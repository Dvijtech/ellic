Ellic uses an elliptical walking linkage.

The mechanism converts continuous rotary motion
into a closed step trajectory.

The foot path approximates a walking step.

![WALKING MECHANISM](media/walking_mechanism.gif)

Frame
Main crank
Connecting rod

The rotating crank drives a connecting rod.

The linkage constrains the motion of the foot element,
producing an approximately elliptical trajectory.

During the lower part of the trajectory the foot
remains close to the ground, generating propulsion.

Typical parameters:

step length
step height
ground contact duration
crank radius
link lengths
