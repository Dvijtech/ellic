# ELLIC

### Open Locomotion Platform & Future MechSport League

**Ellic** is an open research platform exploring a new class of human-scale locomotion mechanisms.

Our long-term dream is ambitious:

> Build a global **mech sport league** as exciting and profitable as Formula-style racing — powered by open robotics and human-piloted machines.

The Ellic platform is the first step.

It combines mechanical locomotion, robotics, VR interaction, and competitive gameplay into a new form of sport and engineering culture.

# 🚀 What You Are Building

Ellic is a **human-scale walking machine** driven by an **elliptical linkage mechanism**.

It behaves somewhere between:

- 🛴 a scooter  
- 🚶 a walking machine  
- 🤖 a mech-like vehicle  

This is not just transport — it is a **mechanical platform + experimental interface system**.

---

# ⚠️ Important Warning

This is a **mechanically active experimental system**.

Risks include:

- moving linkages ⚙️  
- structural loads 🏗️  
- electric drive systems ⚡  

👉 Build only if you understand:
- mechanical systems
- basic electronics
- safe testing practices

Always test:
- at **low speed**
- with **support**
- with **emergency stop ready**

---

# 🧠 How the Build Actually Happens

The build is **not linear engineering perfection**.

In reality, it looks like this:

👉 Mechanics first  
👉 Parts sourcing in parallel  
👉 Electronics later  
👉 Software last  

---

# 🔧 1. Mechanical Fabrication (Core Stage)

This is **80% of the real work**.

### Required tools:

- angle grinder (minimum viable) 🪚  
- tube cutting + bending tools 🔩  
- welding setup 🔥  
- (optional but ideal) laser cutter ✂️  

### Materials:

- steel tubes (main structure)
- standard bearings (used as-is)

💡 Design principle:
> Bearings are selected to fit directly into standard tubes  
(no complex machining required)

---

# ⚙️ 2. 🚲 Bike Components & Machined Parts (Outsourced or DIY)


You will need:

- standart bike components
- simple shafts  
- connection axles  

These are:

- 🟢 cheap  
- 🟢 simple  
- 🟢 easy to outsource  

👉 You can order them from a local machinist.
👉 Option:
- source yourself  
- or order a **pre-selected kit from the project. just ask me somhere here**

---

# 🔩 3. Laser-Cut Parts (Highly Recommended)

For:

- brackets  
- mounts  
- structural connectors  

👉 A **laser cutter** significantly improves:

- precision  
- assembly speed  
- rigidity  

Without it:
- you can improvise, but accuracy will drop

---

# 🧱 4. Optional: 🎨 Body & Styling 3D Printing

- covers  
- visual design  
- plywood 🪵  
- foam panels 🧩  
- composite / carbon panels  
Not required for function, but important for experience.

---

# 🔌 5. 🔗 Wiring & Electronics Assembly 

After mechanics works — add electronics.

### Core components:

- ⚡ 2× motor wheel drivers  
- 🔋 battery (36V, 4–8Ah)  
- 🔽 voltage step-down converter  
- 🧠 ESP32 controller (WiFi + BLE)  
- 🎮 joystick  

Everything is mounted directly on the framу CONNECT:

- motor drivers → motor wheels  
- battery → drivers  
- ESP32 → control signals  
- joystick → ESP32  

---

# 🧪 6. First Functional Mode (Scooter Mode)

At this stage: 👉 The machine works as a **drive platform**

Control logic:

- joystick → ESP32  
- ESP32 → motor drivers  
- motor wheels → motion  

You now have:

🟢 a controllable vehicle  
🟢 basic mobility  
🟢 testable system  

---

# 🤖 7. Mech Mode (Extended System)

Now the interesting part.

You can connect:

- 🥽 VR headset  
- 🎮 same joystick  
- 💻 Unreal Engine system (from repo)

Result:

👉 The machine becomes part of a **mixed-reality mech system**

You can:

- control movement physically  
- control a mech avatar  
- interact in AR/VR space  

---

# 🎯 8. MechSport Layer

With full setup:

- 🎮 player controls mech  
- 🚶 machine mirrors motion  
- 🔫 virtual weapons controlled by joystick  

---

# 🧭 Build Summary (Reality)

Real build flow:

1. 🔧 fabricate frame  
2. ⚙️ assemble linkage  
3. 🧱 install machined parts  
4. 🚲 add bike components  
5. 🔩 mount brackets (laser-cut)  
6. 🔌 install electronics  
7. 🎮 test scooter mode  
8. 🤖 connect VR system  

---

# 💰 Cost & Time

Typical prototype:

- $3,000 – $7,000 (DIY build)
Prebuilt system:
- ~$20,000  
- solo builder: **2-4 months**

# ⚙️ Ellic Walking Mechanism

## 🧭 Overview

**Ellic** uses an **elliptical walking linkage** to create a human-scale locomotion system.

The mechanism converts **continuous rotary motion** into a **closed stepping trajectory**.

Instead of rolling like a wheel, the foot follows a path that **approximates a walking step**.

This creates a motion somewhere between:

- 🛴 a scooter  
- 🤖 a walking robot  
- 🦿 a mechanical exoskeleton vehicle  

# 🔁 Motion of the Mechanism 

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dvijtech/ellic/main/media/walking_mechanism.gif" alt="View Ellic Model" width="40%">
</p>

The video above shows the **core motion principle** of the Ellic walking mechanism.

A rotating crank drives a linkage that moves the foot along an elliptical path.

---
# 🧩 Main Mechanical Elements

The basic mechanism consists of several key components:

- 🧱 **Frame** — structural base of the machine  
- ⚙️ **Main crank** — rotating element driven by the motor  
- 🔗 **Connecting rod** — transfers motion from the crank to the linkage  

Together these elements form the **elliptical walking linkage**.

---

# ⚙️ How the Mechanism Works

1️⃣ The **main crank rotates** continuously.  

2️⃣ The crank **drives the connecting rod**.  

3️⃣ The linkage constrains the motion of the foot element.  

4️⃣ The foot follows an **approximately elliptical trajectory**.

This trajectory produces a **step-like ground interaction** rather than pure rolling motion.

The result is a **stable stepping locomotion pattern** suitable for a human-scale vehicle.

---

# 📏 Typical Kinematic Parameters

The behavior of the mechanism is determined by several geometric parameters:

- ⏱ **wheels size**  
- 📐 **step length** 
- 📐 **hands width**  
 - 🔩 **link lengths**

Changing these parameters alters:

- walking smoothness  
- stability  
- propulsion efficiency  
- rider comfort

# 🚀 Start an Ellic Team

To participate in the first **mech sport experiments** you need a small team.

## 👥 Minimum team structure

1. **🔧 Fabrication Engineer**  
   Responsible for mechanical assembly, repair and mechanical modifications.  

2. **🧠 XR Engineer**  
   Responsible for control systems, firmware and VR interaction.  

3. **🎥 Media Producer**  
   Responsible for filming, editing and publishing team content.  

Teams can be as small as **2–3 people**.  
One person may combine several roles.

# 🌍 Who Might Start an Ellic Team

Different kinds of builders and operators may see themselves in this project.

## 🏎 Karting Track Owners

If you run a karting track or motorsport facility, you already have most of the infrastructure needed for mech sport.

You likely already have:

- mechanical workshops  
- mechanics and technicians  
- competitive drivers  
- a racing culture  

---

## 🥽 VR Arena Owners

- a new attraction for visitors  
- a competition format for young players  
- a basis for educational programs combining engineering and gameplay

---

## 📺 Tech YouTubers and Streamers

If you are a **tech YouTuber, maker, or streamer**, Ellic can become a powerful content project.

The platform combines:

- XR gameplay  
- robotics  
- bionic locomotion machines  
- experimental sport  

Building and piloting an Ellic machine is both a **technical challenge** and a **content story**.

Streamers and creators can build their own teams and document the process for their audiences.

---

## 🎨 Designers and Visual Creators

Experimental machines become much more powerful when they have strong visual identity.

Teams may include:

- industrial designers  
- concept artists  
- 3D artists  
- visual creators  

Designers can develop:

- machine body kits  
- visual concepts for mech sport machines

These visual elements help transform machines into **recognizable competitors and media characters**.

---

## 🛠 Individual Pro

Want to participate but don’t yet have a team?

You can register yourself to **find teammates** for a new project.

📧 Write to: **dselog@gmail.com**

Use the subject: find teammates
**Nickname:**

# 🏁 CORE TEAMS

Early teams will become the **core teams of the first mech sport league**.

The long-term vision is similar to modern motorsport leagues such as **Formula 1 racing**, where teams compete with their own machines while participating in a shared media and competition ecosystem.


## 💰 League Economics (Concept)

The long-term economic model of the league is to share revenue with core teams:

* 🎬 media content and distribution  
* 🎮 live events  
* 🤝 brands & integrations  

to support the growth of both the teams and the championship ecosystem.

The project has gone through many prototype generations exploring different mechanical solutions, drive systems, and ergonomics.
